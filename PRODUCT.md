# Product

PowerLab helps university students build intuition about electrical engineering through explanations, visualizations, calculations, and eventually simulations and guided exercises.

The platform belongs to no course or curriculum. Three-phase systems, transformers, transmission lines, and electric machines are initial independent examples. Future areas may include controls, electromagnetic fields, and power electronics.

The foundation release provides navigation and clearly marked module previews. Future releases should deliver one small, verified learning experience at a time. Project saving/loading and sharing data between related modules are planned. Accounts, mandatory cloud services, AI integration, and desktop packaging are outside the current scope.

Success means students can find a topic, understand assumptions and units, explore an example, and trust the result. Maintain accessible navigation, readable typography, responsive layouts, and both themes as features grow.
